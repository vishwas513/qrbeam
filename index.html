qrbeam

Send a large body of text from one screen to a phone, using an animated QR code. No network, no account: the text travels through the camera and never leaves your devices.

Send →
Paste text or open a .txt file. It plays as an animated QR code.
Receive →
Point your camera at the animation until it fills up. Then copy, share or save the text.

How it works

The text is compressed, split into chunks and sent as a stream of QR frames. After the first pass, every frame is a random mix of chunks (a fountain code), so the receiver can rebuild the text from any frames it catches, not specific ones. Missed or blurry frames just mean you hold the camera a moment longer.

Rough capacity: 1,000 words of English ≈ 2 KB compressed ≈ 3–4 frames. A 200 KB text file goes across in about 10–15 seconds.

On iPhone

Open Receive in Safari and allow the camera. For offline use, tap Share → Add to Home Screen once; it then works with no connection.
